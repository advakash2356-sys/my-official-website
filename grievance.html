2. How to File a Grievance

You can report any violation of our terms, privacy breaches, driver misbehavior, or safety concerns by:

  • Sending a message on WhatsApp to the Grievance Officer.
  • Calling the Grievance Officer directly on the provided contact number.

Please provide the following details when filing a complaint:

  • Your full name and contact number used during booking.
  • Booking ID (e.g., DGC-XXXXXX).
  • Date and time of the incident.
  • A detailed description of the grievance or complaint.

3. Resolution Timeline

As mandated by Indian law, DevgharCabs strictly adheres to the following timelines:

  • Acknowledgment: Your complaint will be acknowledged within 24 hours of receipt.
  • Resolution: The grievance will be resolved within 15 days from the date of its receipt.
  • Safety/Emergency Complaints: Any complaint regarding physical safety or emergency situations will receive immediate priority handling.

4. Data Privacy Requests (DPDP Act 2023)

If your grievance relates to the handling of your personal data, you may exercise your rights under the DPDP Act 2023 by contacting the Grievance Officer. This includes requests for:

  • Access to your personal data.
  • Correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten").
