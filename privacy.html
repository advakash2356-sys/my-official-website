How DevgharCabs handles your personal data — DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

📅 Effective Date: June 1, 2024 | Last Updated: June 2024

3. Legal Basis for Processing (DPDP Act 2023)

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases as defined by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023:

  • Consent: You provide explicit consent when submitting the booking form by ticking the consent checkbox.
  • Legitimate Interest: Connecting passengers with drivers to fulfill the booked cab service.
  • Legal Obligation: Maintaining records as required by applicable law.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To assign a verified driver to your booking
  • To send you booking confirmation via WhatsApp
  • To send your driver's contact details via WhatsApp
  • To contact you in case of any issues with your booking
  • To allow Raja Bhaiya to manage and monitor the platform

5. Who We Share Data With

Your data is shared only with:

  • Your assigned driver (name and phone number only)
  • Raja Bhaiya (platform operator) for booking management

We do NOT share data with: third-party advertisers, data brokers, government agencies (unless legally required), or any other third parties.

6. WhatsApp Communication

By providing your mobile number and booking a cab, you consent to receiving WhatsApp messages related to your booking (confirmation, driver details, status updates). You may opt out at any time by messaging us "STOP" on WhatsApp.

7. Your Rights under DPDP Act 2023

As a data principal, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("Right to be Forgotten")
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: File a complaint with us or the Data Protection Board of India
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate someone to exercise rights on your behalf

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: +91 76430 81977 or WhatsApp

8. Data Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your data:

  • Data stored locally in your browser (localStorage) — not on external servers for Phase 1
  • No passwords stored in plain text
  • Access to admin dashboard protected by PIN
  • WhatsApp end-to-end encryption for communications

9. Cookies

DevgharCabs uses only essential cookies and browser localStorage to:

  • Remember your language preference (Hindi/English)
  • Maintain your booking session
  • Admin dashboard authentication (session only)

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics cookies that identify individual users.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with the revised effective date. Continued use of our service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
